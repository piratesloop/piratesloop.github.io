Delete your Dregfall account

  1. In Dregfall, go to Settings → Account deletion → Copy deletion code.
  2. Email your latest code to - piratesloop4@gmail.com

Already uninstalled or lost the code? Email us so we can check whether we can verify your account.

What we delete

After verifying your request, we delete your Firebase sign-in and your profile, characters, Arena rating and match history from the live game database. Other players’ results may remain with your details removed. We’ll email you when deletion is complete.

OVHcloud’s standard backup is kept for 24 hours. Manual backups, a hashed profile reference and the deletion record have no automatic expiry. Support emails stay until manually deleted.
